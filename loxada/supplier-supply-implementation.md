# Supplier supply management

This implementation follows the [V1.0 business baseline](https://github.com/hilocarloarmenion03/loxadaentertainmentscompany/blob/main/knowledge/specification/v1.0.md#L227) and the existing [supplier panel plan](supplier-work-panels.md). The first release covers private preparation, supplier intake, exact-revision receipts and clarification. Each department owns its operational records and determines which additional authorized workflow its suppliers can access.

Proposed amounts are integer PHP centavos. Date-only quote and offered-rights windows use Manila calendar days and an exclusive end. These initial fields record the supplier’s proposal and protected references. Typed legal, asset-manifest and commercial evidence is validated by the later authority and delivery workflows.

For Manga creators, **New title** starts a private draft with the title, content type, language and format. **Rights for review** is a separate step for contributor roles, rights holders and the proposed publication scope. Supporting references can be added when available. Both steps are saved to the same versioned record.

## Roles, revisions and receipts

1. Reuse the existing portal account, role and original application. An invited supplier account also requires its completed relationship. A session, account or application suspension blocks writes.
2. Department staff opens **Suppliers → Supply submissions → Supply qualification** and inspects the exact verified application. A separate scoped decision records the current application revision, independent reviewer, assessment and protected evidence reference. Old onboarding labels alone supply no decision. A grant can be revoked. A changed application revision invalidates the earlier qualification.
3. Keep one stable supplier work record with append-only numbered revisions. Bind each to the original application, current application revision at capture, and any required qualification and invitation. Keep supplier-qualified external identities and independent receipt history. The supplier can reopen a draft, view older versions, or prepare a replacement for the latest submitted record. A submission attempt never rewrites an earlier receipt.
4. Staff can see submitted versions under **Supply submissions**. Clarification is a separate outcome against the latest exact submitted version. The supplier sees it when opening that version. Draft contents remain private. The workflow records evaluation intake without creating rights, stock, payment, purchase authority, a contract, an award or an analytics event.
5. Supplier actions derive account identity on the server. The database rechecks active role, original object, current revision and invitation scope in the transaction. Retry of an identical change returns its existing receipt. Concurrent conflicts, another supplier’s IDs, invalid payloads and closed invites are rejected. Private schemas keep draft details, roles and authority evidence outside the browser Data API.

Commissioned proposals use a protected tender handoff. The department’s trusted workflow owner may record a real invitation against an active intake qualification and the exact disclosed brief with approval, authority, opening and expiry evidence. Merchandise also requires rights evidence. Staff supplier onboarding invitations cannot substitute for project tenders. The current application supplies no authorized tender issuer, so the supplier panel shows no open proposals until that source exists. This phase has no protected file uploads or supplier-side delivery, PO or project-execution actions.

## Next delivery sequence

1. **Manga.** Derive exact work/edition identities and rights decisions from one authorized creator declaration. Bind signed terms and grant, accept a protected chapter manifest where submission permission permits, validate, review, publish and record an authorized reading attempt. Reconcile the resulting Manila cohort to Title Engagement. Then add publisher batch corrections, independent catalog mapping and publisher attributions. [Manga implementation design](manga-reader-schema.md)
2. **Anime.** Complete offer review and a verified license for one episode, then protect media delivery and editorial readiness. Build the commissioned tender issuer from approved mandates and briefs, then prime selection, signed terms, notice to proceed and milestone/final deliveries. Viewer and financial measures follow their respective sources.
3. **Merchandise.** Complete authenticity review, delegated purchase, supplier PO acknowledgement and shipment, independent inspection and stock movement. Then add the approved manufacturing tender issuer, contract workflow, sample approval, batch milestones and inspection.
4. **Reporting.** Treat submission, approval, access, placement, custody and payment as distinct facts. Only authorized publication/delivery, real reading or playback, and agreed accounting events feed each existing measure. HQ receives separately approved aggregate batches. [Reporting evidence map](reporting-data-map.md)

## Acceptance checks

- Sign in as each supplier role, confirm its existing panel and correct tabs, and test the empty and blocked states.
- Save, reopen, edit and submit a named record. Verify any current qualification, exact invitation and application versions. Check immutable history, identical retries and simultaneous/stale changes.
- View the submitted revision as authorized staff, request clarification and reopen the response as the originating supplier. Try a different supplier, customer, pending onboarding invitation, revoked intake qualification and expired project invitation.
- Validate source identity collisions, date windows, category, positive quantities/MOQ and PHP centavo parsing. Keep test records isolated from department production and analytics.
- Before enabling real licensed media, individual customer data or external provider actions, record the applicable authorization and retention controls. V1.0 accepts the classroom business baseline and separately governs activation of real data and integrations.
