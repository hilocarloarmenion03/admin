# Loxada reporting data map

**Status** · Proposed design for review · November 19, 2026

This map connects the three operational systems and their local analytics to the Super Admin dashboard. It inventories the eight accepted PA06-R2 reports and 34 measures. The [locked V1.0 specification](https://github.com/hilocarloarmenion03/loxadaentertainmentscompany/blob/adfd833fb60515ccf78a684778e75c6df91429cf/knowledge/specification/v1.0.md) governs the business rules. Proposed physical design choices are marked in the [Manga reader schema](manga-reader-schema.md).

Account Analysis counts current account records using their registration or role-assignment dates. Supplier Activity uses current invitation and application statuses and compares recent intake windows. Invitation acceptance and setup timestamps help answer some milestone questions. A current application row and its last review timestamp cannot establish every historical transition or its first review. If historical Supplier Activity is added, inventory existing evidence, capture missing milestones prospectively, and state the earliest reliable date. ## From operational evidence to local analytics

1. **Record the business event in its department.** Keep stable source event identity, department and source process, record version, UTC occurrence and recorded times, actor and protected evidence references, correction lineage, and fixture marker where the V1.0 source envelope applies. Preserve provider namespaces and source identifiers. Cash, obligation, service, delivery, custody and approval remain independently evidenced.
2. **Transform in the owning department.** Proposed first implementation uses a scheduled incremental batch plus explicit reruns. Use recorded time with a stable tie-breaker or another proven checkpoint for discovery. Use the business date named by the measure for grouping. Validate types, required fields, ownership and references. Preserve exact retries as one effect, quarantine conflicting duplicate identities, and keep unmatched observations for reconciliation. Rebuild affected partitions after late events or corrections. Keep the last verified publication and its freshness when a refresh fails.
3. **Reconcile, model, and serve the local mart.** Create one star per fact grain, then a galaxy where facts truly share dimensions. Model supported hierarchies such as calendar or work/edition/release in the snowflake view. Conform dimension definitions as appropriate and keep roles such as occurrence date, accounting date and cash date explicit. Pre-aggregate compatible facts before comparing them. Apply slice, dice, drill down/up, pivot, and authorized local drill-through under the [OLAP standard](olap-reporting.md).
4. **Respect how each measure aggregates.** Sum compatible additive movements. Use the appropriate point-in-time value for snapshots. Compute distinct accounts over the authorized underlying population, or show the roll-up as unavailable if the evidence or approved grain cannot support it. Recalculate rates from their components. An unknown value, a withheld value, a true zero and a stale verified result each have distinct meanings.

Each feature’s acceptance package records the V1.0 process and metric IDs, its source schema and action that writes the evidence, the population and reporting window, exclusions, reconciliation test, and permission tests. Use labelled fictional inputs for the classroom demonstration, with fixture inclusion clearly scoped. Analytics work includes source capture from the first feature rather than depending on retrospective reconstruction. ## HQ warehouse and recommendation cases

**Department OLTP → local evidence → department ETL and mart → exact approved aggregate batch → HQ validation and publication → Super Admin BI.**

- **Export and load.** Implement the `CG-13 → CG-14 → CG-16 → CG-17` chain. Identify the source department, dataset and definition version, partition and grain, immutable revision and content digest, permitted columns and exact approval, as-of time, and reconciliation evidence. Validate authority and the exact manifest on intake and recheck permission at publication. Atomically replace the complete approved partition, including a valid complete empty replacement. Record retries and conflicts independently. Preserve the previous verified revision when a load fails or an incoming batch is held.
- **Privacy and retention.** Share only permitted aggregates and minimized case evidence. Use separate department-scoped customer accounts. Under V1.0, positive customer-derived cohorts below ten distinct local accounts require withholding the whole affected row and derived values. Check complementary totals, overlapping slices and manifest metadata for disclosure by differencing. Department activity, contacts, raw payment data and protected source proof stay local. Apply retention by purpose, including the 90-day raw reading/watch window and 24-calendar-month approved aggregate/manifest policy.
- **Meaningful comparisons.** A common department and Manila calendar can align compatible summaries. A monthly billing entitlement and a daily physical stock balance retain their distinct meanings. HQ cannot derive provider-, episode-, customer- or other finer-grained results from summaries that omit those attributes. Keep rights to view HQ staff accounts separate from approval and publication authority.
- **Manga → Anime.** Under `M-A-13` and the applicable Central/Anime contracts, support a reviewed feasibility case referencing the work, precise cohort and period, approved evidence revision and as-of time, rationale, and rights-inquiry limits. Record the actual Central intake, Owner feasibility outcome, and departmental receipt separately. Anime still checks adaptation rights and secures the independent finance, contract and stage decisions required for execution.
- **Anime → Merch.** Under `CG-09`, `CG-10` and applicable Merch contracts, bind approved viewing evidence to the source title and separate feasibility and investment decisions. Merch checks demand, product-class manufacture and sale permissions, and other applicable rights and terms. Use Steward-reviewed effective IP mappings where available. An unmapped title is still a valid local observation, and multi-IP tags do not duplicate whole project or SKU values.
