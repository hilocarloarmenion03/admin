# Loxada business intelligence
## Executive evidence review · 26 November 2026

The first decision is whether a Manga title has enough evidenced readership to warrant an adaptation feasibility review. A subsequent Anime audience review can support a merchandise inquiry. Each investment has its own decision and evidence.

---

## What the opening audit established

Manga’s reading cube was deployed. Its production tables held zero reading attempts and zero monthly loads. Anime had zero recorded paid periods. All three department catalogs had zero entries. HQ reported local staff counts.

Demand, profitability and conversion remain unmeasured in this snapshot. A missing month must be visible to the person making a decision.

---

## What the executive will see

| Department | Question | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Manga | Which title attracted and retained readers in the period? | Eligible starts, exact completions and distinct readers |
| Anime | Which title sustained viewing during paid service? | Eligible watch intervals, completions and separate subscription evidence |
| Merchandise | Which SKUs sold profitably and can be replenished? | Delivered units, approved returns, matched lot costs and available stock |

HQ can compare authorized summaries with matching periods and definitions. The departments retain their detailed operational records.

---

## Evidence needed for a decision

1. Deliver an authorized chapter and reconcile measured reading activity to the Manga cube.
2. Publish an eligible, reconciled aggregate to HQ with its scope, coverage and revision.
3. Review audience evidence alongside rights and feasibility before recording a decision.
4. Apply the same source-to-report check to Anime viewing and Merchandise delivery.
